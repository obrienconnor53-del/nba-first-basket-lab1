What is automated — and what isn't

Automated now

Today's game schedule; historical game lookup; first made field-goal detection from play-by-play; player rankings; CSV export.

Not automated yet

Confirmed starters; injury adjustments; tip-off player matchups; reliable first-basket sportsbook odds; calibrated model training; verified betting recommendations.

Method: a first basket means the first successful field goal, not the first free throw. We inspect historical completed games and use a smoothed estimate of first-scorer frequency. Historical team tendencies are combined with a neutral 50/50 team prior; this is an intentionally simple baseline, not the promised full opening-possession simulation.

Data courtesy of publicly accessible ESPN endpoints (unofficial and undocumented). Not affiliated with ESPN or the NBA. Never rely on unverified probabilities for wagering.
