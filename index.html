What is automated — and what isn't

Automated now

Today's game schedule; historical game lookup; first made scoring-event detection from play-by-play; player rankings; CSV export.

Not automated yet

Confirmed starters; injury adjustments; tip-off player matchups; automatic first-basket sportsbook odds; calibrated model training; verified betting recommendations.

Historical model definition: earliest identifiable scoring play, including made free throws where ESPN reports them. The two seasons are displayed separately and current-season results receive more weight. We do not yet verify player starts or roster continuity; counts are team games analysed, not player starts. Missing games are excluded, not counted as misses. This is a heuristic research baseline, not a calibrated opening-possession simulation.

Data courtesy of publicly accessible ESPN endpoints (unofficial and undocumented). Not affiliated with ESPN or the NBA. Never rely on unverified probabilities for wagering.
