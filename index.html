What is automated — and what isn't

Automated now

Today's game schedule; historical game lookup; first made scoring-event detection; first-shot identification; opening-action text tags; opponent first-shot concessions proxy; player rankings; CSV export.

Not automated yet

Confirmed starters; injury adjustments; actual tip-off player matchups; verified coach-designed set plays; automatic first-basket sportsbook odds; calibrated model training; verified betting recommendations.

Historical model definition: earliest identifiable scoring play, including made free throws where ESPN reports them. First-shot and set-play tags are inferred from play-by-play descriptions, NOT verified coaching instructions. Opponent opening defence is a limited proxy based on the first conceded shot. The two seasons are displayed separately and current-season results receive more weight. We do not yet verify player starts or roster continuity; counts are team games analysed, not player starts. Missing games are excluded, not counted as misses. This is a heuristic research baseline, not a calibrated opening-possession simulation.
